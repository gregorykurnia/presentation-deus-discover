DEUS pitch deck: three directions

Each direction is shown on three real slides from the brief: the title, the Telkom proof slide, and the "two kinds of fit" core idea. The text is the final copy. Yellow highlights mark [placeholders]. The logo and Telkom photo aren't in the repo yet, so they appear as labeled boxes.

A · Daybreak B · Game Pieces C · Clear Signal

A. Daybreak

Soft morning-light gradients, a confident grotesque, and two overlapping colour discs that stand for role fit and culture fit.

#0F1A3A #2D5BFF #FF6F4E #D3E2FF #FFDCCB
Fonts
Bricolage Grotesque (headlines, numbers) and Figtree (body). Modern, slightly characterful, very legible at a distance.
Layout
Left-aligned headline, one big visual on the right. Numbers are huge and in cobalt. Pale glass panels hold only quotes and steps.
Why
Feels fresh and premium but calm, which suits HR directors. The two-disc motif makes "role + culture = one score" the visual idea of the whole deck.

B. Game Pieces

Bright, flat game-board shapes that nod to the product being real games, kept in check by a disciplined grid.

#1B1442 #6B4BFF #1FD1A5 #FFC940 #FF86C8
Fonts
Unbounded (wide, bold headlines) and Plus Jakarta Sans (body). Jakarta was designed for Jakarta's city identity, which is a nice local touch for an Indonesian audience.
Layout
Big blocks of solid colour do the work: a violet side panel, mint and yellow halves meeting at a score dial. Shapes stay at the edges so text keeps a clean field.
Why
The most energetic and memorable option. It signals "this isn't a boring psikotes" right away. It's the boldest of the three, so it carries a little more risk with conservative BUMN rooms.

C. Clear Signal

Bright mint and sky over a faint measurement grid, with an elegant serif. It reads like good science that's easy on the eye.

#062A30 #00B386 #2E9BFF #D4F66A #C6F3E1
Fonts
Instrument Serif (headlines, big numbers) and Geist (body). The serif adds authority and the clean sans keeps it modern.
Layout
Open, airy pages with hairline structure. Numbers sit in a row like readings on an instrument. The core slide is an equation: Role fit + Culture fit = one ring (green half + blue half).
Why
Speaks most directly to the skeptical psikolog. It feels rigorous and trustworthy while staying bright and light. The ring becomes DEUS's recurring "score" symbol.
